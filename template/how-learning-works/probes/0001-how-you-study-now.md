# Probe 0001: how you study now

**What this measures:** your starting habits, so lessons can aim at what will help most. It teaches
nothing and there are no wrong answers. "I don't know" and "never" are useful answers.

**Rules:** answer from memory and honestly. Don't look anything up.

## Questions

1. When you want to remember something you read, what do you usually do afterwards?
2. When did you last test yourself on something without looking at the material? What was it?
3. Think of something you studied hard and then forgot. How did you study it?
4. How long are your usual study sessions, and how often do you have one?
5. What do you want to learn next, and why?

Submit your answers from the dashboard (the probe's **Submit answers** button), then press **✦ Grade** on the dashboard.
