# Resources: how learning works

- **Bjork, R. A., & Bjork, E. L. (2020). Desirable difficulties in theory and practice.** *Journal of
  Applied Research in Memory and Cognition*, 9(4). The storage vs retrieval strength distinction, and
  why harder practice can be better practice.
- **Roediger, H. L., & Karpicke, J. D. (2006). Test-enhanced learning.** *Psychological Science*,
  17(3). The classic testing-effect experiment: rereading wins at five minutes, recall wins at a week.
- **Dunlosky, J., et al. (2013). Improving students' learning with effective learning techniques.**
  *Psychological Science in the Public Interest*, 14(1). Ten techniques rated; practice testing and
  distributed practice come out on top.
- **Brown, P. C., Roediger, H. L., & McDaniel, M. A. (2014). *Make It Stick*.** Harvard University
  Press. The same research, written for learners.
