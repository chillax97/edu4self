# Mission: how learning works (example topic)

## Why
This topic ships with edu4self so a new learner can try the whole loop once, end to end: open a
lesson, answer questions, submit, ask Claude to grade, and watch the dashboard change. It is also
the idea every later lesson is built on: learning is proven by retrieval, not by time spent.

## Success looks like
- You can explain retrieval strength vs storage strength in your own words, without notes.
- You have changed one real study habit for a topic you care about, and can say why.

## Out of scope
- Neuroscience details. This is about what to do, not about brain anatomy.
