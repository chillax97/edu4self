Study moves

What to do instead of rereading. One page, prints well.

Instead ofDo thisWhy
Rereading the chapterClose it, write down everything you remember, then checkRetrieval builds storage strength
HighlightingTurn the heading into a question and answer itGeneration beats recognition
Cramming the night beforeThree short sessions on different daysSpacing: forgetting a little, then recalling, is the point
Doing 20 of the same problemMix problem types in one sessionInterleaving: you practise choosing the method too
Reading the solution when stuckTry for a fixed time, write your best guess, then readA wrong attempt makes the correction stick
Judging by "it feels familiar"Test yourself after a gap of daysFluency now predicts little about later
Rule of thumb. If a study session felt effortless, it probably raised retrieval strength only. Some struggle is the signal, not the problem.
